# Privacy decisions

Your record of privacy choices, especially where you went a different way from the advice in [privacy.md](privacy.md). Claude fills this in with you (it never decides for you),
and re-reads it before raising privacy questions so it doesn't ask again about something you've settled.

Rules of thumb: write the decision **in your own words**; give the **reason**; set a **review date**. Overriding the advice is allowed. Leaving it unexplained is what causes trouble.
